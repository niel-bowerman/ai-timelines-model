SAR has two heights. If there is no explosion, the likeliest reason is that research taste proved hard to automate, so a superhuman researcher needs more progress.

Step 3 is the forecast at the top. The model spreads uncertainty around each median date from step 2, then blends the three regimes by how likely each is.

SAR is a superhuman AI researcher. TED-AI is AI at least as good as top human experts at virtually all cognitive tasks. ASI is superintelligence. The milestone names and definitions are borrowed from the AI Futures Project. Hover over or tap an i for a definition.

Every starting value is a judgement call. None is fitted to data.
