How the model works. Each regime has a curve for the pace of progress. Adding up the pace over time gives capability. A regime reaches a milestone when capability crosses the milestone's height. The model then spreads uncertainty around each crossing date and blends the three regimes by their probabilities.

Every starting value is a judgement call. None is fitted to data.

Milestone names and definitions are borrowed from the AI Futures Project. Hover over or tap an i for a definition.
